Project: TagIt – Collaborative Digital Graffiti Platform
Date: October 2025
Interviewer: Chen Li
Participants:

P1 – Tang (craft hobbyist)

P2 – Li (university student, no art background but enjoys drawing)
Relationship: Couple
Session Duration: 30 minutes
Platform Tested: http://47.121.140.22:3000/

Q1. How was your overall first impression when using TagIt together?

P1: It was fun and surprisingly easy to use. I liked that we could start drawing right away without any setup or sign-up. It felt spontaneous, kind of like doodling together on paper.
P2: Yeah, I agree. Even though I’m not really an artist, it didn’t feel intimidating. The interface was simple, and I liked that I could just start drawing without needing to learn any tools first.

Q2. How did you coordinate with each other while drawing?

P1: We didn’t plan much beforehand. I started with some small shapes on one side, and Li began drawing on the other. Then we started adding to each other’s parts, connecting them into one picture.
P2: It happened naturally. Sometimes she would draw something cute, and I’d add a funny detail next to it. It felt relaxed, like we were having a conversation through drawings.

Q3. Did you feel connected to each other during the drawing session?

P1: Yes, definitely. We were sitting together, watching what each other was doing, and reacting in real time. It made the process feel very social and cooperative.
P2: I liked that we could share the same canvas — it gave us a sense of doing something creative together, not just using separate screens.

Q4. Were there any difficulties or limitations you noticed while using the platform?

P1: The main issue for me was the brush tool options. There’s only one basic brush, so it felt a bit limited after a while. I wanted more sizes or different textures to make the drawing richer.
P2: Yeah, same. I’d like to have more variety — maybe marker, spray, or watercolor effects. It would make it easier for beginners like me to experiment.

Q5. Did you encounter any technical problems or missing features?

P1: I really missed an undo button. When I made a small mistake, I had to erase manually, which interrupted the flow.
P2: And I think a zoom in/out function would be useful. Sometimes we wanted to add details but couldn’t zoom closer, especially on a phone.

Q6. You mentioned using the platform on mobile — how was that experience?

P1: It worked smoothly overall, but when I tried to save my artwork, the delete button didn’t appear because my file name was too long. It was cut off on the screen.
P2: Yeah, I noticed that too. Maybe the layout could adjust automatically or let you scroll horizontally when the text gets too long.

Q7. How did you feel about the social or collaborative aspect overall?

P1: It was really nice! It’s different from drawing alone — it felt like a shared creative moment.
P2: I liked it a lot. Even though I’m not good at art, it was fun to collaborate and react to what Tang drew. It made me feel more involved.

Q8. Did the platform support your creative flow or interrupt it?

P1: It supported it well most of the time. There weren’t any big lags, and the simplicity helped us stay focused.
P2: Yeah, but missing features like undo or zoom made it slightly frustrating when small mistakes happened.

Q9. If you could request new features, what would you want most?

P1: More brush styles for sure — different thicknesses, textures, or even blending tools.
P2: I’d add the undo/redo buttons, zoom function, and fix that mobile save-page layout issue. These would make it much smoother for casual users like me.

Q10. Would you use TagIt again or recommend it to others?

P1: Yes, absolutely. It’s perfect for couples or friends who want to do something creative together, even if they’re far apart.
P2: I’d recommend it too. It’s simple but has great potential for online group activities or just relaxing together.

Q11. Any final thoughts or feelings after using the platform?

P1: I think it’s a very sweet and social tool. It makes art approachable for people who just want to enjoy creativity, not only for professionals.
P2: I agree. It helped us spend time together in a fun way, but I hope future versions can offer a bit more creative freedom with the tools.
