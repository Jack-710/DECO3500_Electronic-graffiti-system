System Usage Feedback 1 (Interviewee A)

Speaker 1:
What was your first reaction when you joined by scanning the QR code and saw that you could draw in real time and project it onto a public screen? Would you try our software?

Speaker 2:
Yes. If someone were to show me, for example, an AI-generated work projected onto the screen, I might be tempted to try it too. Drawing something feels fun and engaging.

Speaker 1:
When drawing on your phone, do you think the color palette and brush selection features meet your needs? Are there any areas that need improvement?

Speaker 2:
First, the phone screen itself is small, making it inconvenient to draw on. The color palette and brush selection are also prone to accidental touches, making it difficult to select the desired color and lacking precision.

If improvements are needed, they could include more brush types, especially finer brushes. Because the screen is small, finer brushes provide more creative space.

Speaker 1:
Okay, I see. If you could see different locations on a map where you could create, would you be willing to go there?

Speaker 2:
Yes. If such a feature were available, it would surely attract fans to draw their idols, or people with similar interests to check in. This would increase the number of works and foster a more creative atmosphere.

Speaker 1:
Our system also features likes and comments. Do you feel pressured when your drawings receive comments?

Speaker 2:
It certainly feels a bit stressful. After all, everyone has different aesthetic tastes, and unkind comments can be discouraging and create negative emotions for the creator.

Speaker 1:
Okay, thank you very much for your feedback!
