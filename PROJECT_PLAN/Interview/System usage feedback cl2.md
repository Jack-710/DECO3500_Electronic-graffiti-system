System Usage Feedback 2 (Interviewee B)

Speaker 1:
Hello, what was your first reaction when you scanned the QR code to join and saw that you could draw in real time and project it onto a public screen?

Speaker 2:
I don't have much objection to this kind of public project. However, I would like to see clearer information about user privacy and data protection upfront, such as not using information for commercial purposes or collecting personal information. From an ethical perspective, I hope this is more thoughtful.

Speaker 1:
Okay. Do you think there's a difference between drawing on a phone and drawing on a sketchpad?

Speaker 2:
Of course there is. Drawing on a phone falls into the realm of hand-drawing, while drawing on a large screen or sketchpad is more traditional. Different people may be more comfortable with different creative methods, so it's best to provide multiple ways to participate so everyone can create in the way they're most comfortable.

Speaker 1:
You can see different creation spots on the map. Would you be interested in visiting these locations to participate?

Speaker 2:
If I happened to pass by, I might give it a try, but I wouldn't go out of my way to seek them out. For me, these kinds of activities are secondary activities, something I can do on the side, like shopping or watching a movie.

Speaker 1:
What do you think if your work receives likes or comments?

Speaker 2:
I think we need to strengthen the review of user comments. It's a good thing people are willing to share, but malicious comments or bad behavior can have a negative impact, which is unacceptable.

Speaker 1:
Our system offers two ways to participate: drawing on a drawing board or drawing on your phone. Which do you prefer?

Speaker 2:
I prefer drawing on a drawing board in person. It's more immersive and engaging. By comparison, the experience of using a mobile app isn't as intense. I prefer drawing on a large screen, both for the graphics and the controls.

Speaker 1:
Okay, thank you very much for participating!
